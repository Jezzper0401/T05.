Overview

As home entertainment displays grow larger and incorporate brighter panel technologies, understanding power consumption becomes vital for consumers managing electricity costs. The visualisations below explore television energy efficiency ratings across display technologies alongside long-term trends in wholesale power prices across Australia (1998–2024).

1. Energy Consumption vs. Star Rating (Scatter Plot)

Displays individual television models. Higher star ratings correlate with lower annual kWh.

2. Spot Electricity Prices 1998–2024 (Line Chart)

Average wholesale power spot prices ($/MWh) across the National Electricity Market (NEM).

3. Mean Energy Consumption for 55" TVs (Bar Chart)

Comparison of mean annual energy consumption (kWh/year) exclusively for popular 55-inch models.

4. Mean Energy Footprint by Screen Tech (Donut Chart)

Share of mean energy consumption across LCD, LED, and OLED across all TV sizes combined.
